Live refresh runs on every runtime nifra serves. `@nifrajs/web/live-redis` adds `redisLiveBus()`, an ordered log on Redis Streams that many processes share; a reader that falls behind a trim reports the gap, and every socket that held a watermark from before it resyncs. `@nifrajs/web/live-hub` adds `liveHub()`, a hibernating Durable Object that owns the log on Cloudflare Workers and holds every live socket: pass its namespace to `live({ hub })`. The hub keeps revocations and socket membership in storage, answers heartbeats without waking, runs one alarm at the earliest deadline, checks grant expiry before every frame, and refuses calls from an app whose `secret`, `appId` or `grantTtl` differ from its own. It holds 5,000 sockets and 150,000 socket-tag subscriptions by default (`maxSockets`, `maxMemberships`). Both dev servers now serve `app.ws()` routes, the live socket among them, through the route's own `upgrade()` guard and Origin check; under Vite this needs the `ws` package. `buildClient()`, `buildTarget()` and the dev servers take `live: { path, loadPath }` for a live runtime on non-default paths.
